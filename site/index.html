How we work

Six stages, each one approved by you.

  1. 01

    Briefing

    We agree what the work is for: the product, the audience, the formats, and the references you love.

  2. 02

    Creative direction

    We set the look before anything is generated: mood, styling, light, locations, camera.

  3. 03

    AI development

    We build the pieces the campaign needs: the models, the environments, and your product.

  4. 04

    Content generation

    We produce the images and sequences, testing and refining until the whole set is consistent.

  5. 05

    Refinement

    Compositing, retouching, colour and clean-up. This is where a render becomes a finished asset.

  6. 06

    Delivery

    You receive the approved files in the formats and specs your channels need.

Feedback is part of every stage, and nothing moves forward without your sign-off. If something already approved needs to change later, such as the model, we go back to that stage and quote it separately.

Good to know

Questions we hear first.

What do you need from us?

Product photos or packaging artwork, your brand guidelines, and where the work will run. Nothing new needs to be shot.

Will the product look exactly like ours?

Yes. Silhouette, print, colour, hardware and logo placement are matched to your reference. Everything around the product is ours to invent.

Do we see the direction before you produce?

Yes. Each stage is reviewed and approved before the next begins, starting with the creative direction.

How do revisions work?

Feedback is included at every stage. A change to something you have already approved may mean regenerating earlier work, so we quote that separately.

Can you match our brand’s tone?

That is the brief. For Dreamies it meant a stylised, comic film. For Escudo, a slow cinematic one.

Which formats do you deliver?

Whatever your channels need. Films and stills in any ratio, composed for the placement instead of cropped to fit.

Who will we be working with?

A small studio in Braga, Portugal. You talk to the person making the work, from the first message to the final file.
